I build systems where people own their identity, data, computation, and property. No blind trust. No intermediaries deciding what you can access or what happens to what's yours.

That means minimizing unnecessary trust, preserving privacy, and making every critical assumption explicit, verifiable, and replaceable.

I don't marry technologies. I study them, combine them, replace them when the architecture demands better. The principle never moves: give people more control over the systems they depend on.

I build for the next generation. I know what it feels like to lack validation early on, and I refuse to let that be the default. Hiring me isn't just about skills. It's about philosophy, vision, and the parts of me AI cannot replicate.

---

### 🧠 How I build: Vericoding

I don't outsource the architecture to AI. I use AI extensively as an implementation and exploration multiplier, but I keep the reasoning, specifications, constraints, and verification under human control.

<div align="center">
  <br>
  <b>AI can help me write the code.</b><br>
  <i>It doesn't get to decide what the system means.</i>
  <br><br>
</div>
